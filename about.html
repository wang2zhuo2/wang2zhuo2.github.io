I'm Zonen Wang

(Zhuoneng Wang)

Senior product designer for XR, spatial interaction, and multimodal input.

I've spent nine years in XR, turning new ways of interacting into products people can use. For the past four years at Microsoft, I've designed for Mesh and Teams, from immersive meetings and events to spatial interaction on Meta Quest and Apple Vision Pro.

Across Mesh and Teams, I design how people meet, present, and move in 3D. I unified spatial navigation, shipped immersive Q&A and call-on-stage, owned onboarding, and prototyped AI-assisted Q&A moderation. I also led controller-free hand interaction for Meta Quest and Apple Vision Pro. It shipped in Mesh on Quest, and the gesture system and hand cursor are patented. I prototype in Unity and contribute interaction code.

Before Microsoft, I studied interaction design at Carnegie Mellon, where I led the multisensory interaction for BEYOND. Earlier, as Senior PM and Designer at ByteDance, I led TikTok Effect House from concept to MVP with 40+ engineers, including its node-based visual programming system.

I learned XR by building it. At RIOS, I developed Hand MR, bare-hand tools for architects and interior designers, and shipped VR and AR experiences. At Kilograph, I built iOS AR apps and interactive 3D web experiences. My roots are in architecture at UCLA, where I co-built Echo of Brain, a brainwave-driven installation.

The thread through it all: making new forms of interaction understandable, usable, and buildable.
